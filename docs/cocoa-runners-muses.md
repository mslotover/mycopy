# The four Cocoa Runners muses

## Angela

Worried well off. Older Metrocultural. 58. Listens to the Zoe podcast, does yoga, eats fermented foods. Very concerned with health and wellbeing. Cares about polyphenols and heavy metals. Wants to learn and educate, and looking for foods she can trust. Enjoys a few squares of dark chocolate after dinner, eats alone or with her partner. Likes to gift it to get friends into craft chocolate. She's a subscriber and isn't fussed about packaging. She reads the emails occasionally.

## Stefan

Artisan Aficionado. 37. Millennial. Buys specialty coffee, craft beer, kombucha. Visits cool restaurants and likes to be on top of the cool foodie scene. Interested in flavour, very attracted to good packaging and the aesthetic side of things. Spends time on Instagram. A bit hipster. Cares about ethical, but that's not the main concern. He likes Bare Bones and Taza. He has subscribed but doesn't at the moment. He doesn't read our emails but does follow us on Instagram. James Hoffmann fan. DINK.

## Flavour enthusiasts – Holly

Loyal subscriber. Lives in London, really nerdy about coffee and chocolate. Really cares about flavour. Judge at Great Taste. In the foodie world professionally. Would attend the masterclass.

## Henry

Banker/lawyer, 40s or 50s, kids moving out. Goes to see classical music. Members of the Tate, go to concerts at the Barbican, wealthy gifter. Would like to be classy. Very into art. Possibly member of a private club. Enjoys fine wine. Has a subscription but doesn't pay much attention, likes to gift well and look fancy.

## Bonus: Helena – flavour and chocolate

Cacao superfan. 43. Wears cacao jewellery, attends cacao and chocolate events, may be a judge for awards event. Visits farms and factories when she travels. She knows lots of brands and is interested in new things. Very passionate about the ethical side and sourcing. Wants to campaign and spread the word. Subscriber, but sometimes prefers to order her own bars.
