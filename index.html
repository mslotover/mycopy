MyCopy by Meret

Copy, back up and sync your Google Drive files.

MyCopy is a tool that runs on your own computer. It copies files between your Google Drive and your local storage, so you can keep backups and move files in bulk. It talks to Google directly and never sends your files to anyone else.

How it works Privacy Policy

What MyCopy does

⬇️

Back up Drive

Download a copy of your Google Drive files and folders to your computer or an external disk.

⬆️

Upload in bulk

Copy large folders from your computer into Google Drive in one step, keeping the folder structure.

🔄

Keep in sync

Update only the files that changed, so your local copy and your Drive stay matched.

How it works

  1. 1

    Sign in with Google

    You sign in with Google and grant MyCopy permission to access your Google Drive.

  2. 2

    Choose what to copy

    Pick the Drive folders and local folders you want to copy or sync between.

  3. 3

    MyCopy does the transfer

    Files move directly between your computer and Google Drive over an encrypted connection.

How MyCopy uses your Google data

MyCopy requests access to your Google Drive so it can list, read, create and update the files you choose to copy or sync. That access is used only to carry out the transfers you ask for.

  • Everything runs on your own computer. Your files and access tokens are not sent to or stored on any Meret server.
  • Your data is not sold, shared, used for advertising or used to train AI models.
  • You can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

MyCopy's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Read the full Privacy Policy.

Questions

Who is MyCopy for?

Anyone who wants a reliable way to back up their Google Drive, or to move a lot of files in or out of it.

Does Meret see my files?

No. MyCopy connects straight from your computer to Google. Your files never pass through Meret.

How do I stop MyCopy accessing my Drive?

Remove it at myaccount.google.com/permissions, then delete it from your computer.
