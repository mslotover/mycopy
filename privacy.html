Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

MyCopy ("the app") is operated by Meret ("we", "us", "our"). This policy explains what information the app accesses, how it is used, and the choices you have.

Summary

  • MyCopy runs entirely on your own device.
  • It connects directly from your device to Google Drive. Your files and Google account data never pass through or get stored on any server we operate.
  • We do not collect, sell, share or analyse your data.

Information the app accesses

When you sign in with Google and grant permission, MyCopy can access the following through the Google Drive API:

  • Google Drive files and folders: file names, contents and metadata (such as size, type and modification date), as needed to perform the copy and sync operations you ask for.
  • OAuth access tokens: issued by Google so the app can act on your behalf. These are stored only on your device.

How the information is used

Google user data is used only to provide the features you explicitly request, such as listing, copying, uploading or downloading files between your Google Drive and other locations you choose. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or training machine learning or AI models.

Storage and security

All processing happens locally on your device. Access tokens and any configuration are saved in local files on your computer, protected by your operating system's account security. We have no access to them. Data travelling between your device and Google is encrypted with HTTPS/TLS.

Sharing

We do not share, sell, rent or transfer your Google user data to anyone. Because the app does not send your data to us, we have nothing to share.

Google API Services User Data Policy

MyCopy's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Data stays on your device until you remove it. To stop the app accessing your account and delete its data:

  1. Revoke MyCopy's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  2. Delete the app and its local configuration files from your device.

Children

MyCopy is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: info@meret.uk
