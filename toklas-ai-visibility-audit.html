Toklas — Search and AI Visibility Audit

Crawled 14 November 2026 · 6 pages + robots.txt, sitemap, llms.txt, 4 menu PDFs · toklaslondon.com (Wix)
In short: AI tools can reach and read the site, and the FAQ page is unusually good. The biggest problem is a factual one. The site's structured data gives the restaurant's address as 9 Surrey Street, which is the bakery's address. The visible text says the restaurant is at 1 Surrey Street. AI tools and Google read that structured data directly. It also leaves out the opening hours, the cuisine and the bakery.

Already working

  • good robots.txt allows every AI crawler (GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot, Google-Extended and the rest). The only bot blocked is PetalBot.
  • good Page text loads without JavaScript, so crawlers see the real content.
  • good The sitemap exists, is listed in robots.txt and was updated this week.
  • good The FAQ page answers real customer questions in plain language: hours, location, pre-theatre prices, terrace, vegetarian options, events and the bakery. This is the format AI tools quote most easily.
  • good llms.txt exists, and the site has a Wix MCP endpoint that AI agents can query.

H1 headings: on each page, select the main heading (such as "About Toklas" or "Toklas Restaurant FAQs"), open the text settings and set its tag to Heading 1. Use one per page.

5 · Facts and menus

  • Terrace capacity: pick one number and use it everywhere. The homepage and FAQs say about 80. The homepage Events section says 100 outside. The Eater quote saying "100-cover" is fine to leave, because it's a quote.
  • Menus: the Today's Menu, Pre-Theatre, Bar and Wine menus are PDFs. AI tools can read PDFs, but less reliably than page text, and dish names are what people search for ("Mediterranean lunch near the Strand"). A simple Menus page with a few signature dishes and price ranges as text would help. You don't need to put the full daily menu on it.

Off-site: worth checking (I couldn't reach these from here)

  • Google Business Profile for both the restaurant and the bakery. Check the addresses (1 vs 9 Surrey Street), the hours and the phone numbers, and make sure they match the site exactly.
  • SevenRooms, OpenTable, TripAdvisor, The Infatuation, Time Out and Hot Dinners listings. AI tools cite these heavily for restaurant questions. Look for the same address mix-up.
  • Bing Places / Apple Business Connect. ChatGPT search relies on Bing, and Siri and Apple Maps use Apple's data.

Appendix: lower priority

  • 156 of 185 homepage images have no alt text. Most are the press-quote carousel, which repeats in the HTML.
  • The sitemap lists /event-testimonials, but that page returns a 404 error. Remove the page from the sitemap or restore it.
  • The footer still says © 2025.
  • The press quotes appear four times in the homepage HTML, because of the carousel. This is harmless, but it adds page weight (the homepage HTML is about 2.9 MB).

Not yet tested: what AI tools actually say

This audit covers what crawlers can read. It doesn't yet cover what ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini or Google AI Overviews actually say when someone asks for "Mediterranean restaurant near the Strand" or "best bakery near Covent Garden". That test runs through OpenSEO and uses credits. Answers vary between runs, so a monthly repeat shows the trend better than a single check.
