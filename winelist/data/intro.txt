Whether you’re returning to a favourite or discovering something new, we’d be delighted to help you find the right wine.
We’ve built this list with the same spirit that guides our hospitality: generous, curious and without pretence.

This list has been shaped by the growers we admire, the places we’ve visited and the bottles we’ve returned to time and time again. Alongside timeless classics, you’ll find producers quietly rethinking tradition, emerging regions alongside iconic appellations, and familiar grapes shown in new ways. Each bottle has earned its place for its quality, character and ability to express where it comes from.

Many of these producers are people we’ve had the pleasure of visiting, learning from and sharing meals with. Those experiences remind us that behind every bottle is not just a vineyard, but a person, a place and a story worth sharing.
We hope it’s a collection you’ll feel comfortable exploring, whether you know exactly what you’re looking for or would rather let us guide you.

The • symbol highlights wines made with a low-intervention philosophy. They may appear cloudy, lightly sparkling or express flavours outside more familiar styles. We love them for their individuality and are always happy to guide you through them if you’re curious.
